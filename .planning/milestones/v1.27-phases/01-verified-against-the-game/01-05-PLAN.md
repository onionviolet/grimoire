<objective>
Give the ConVar catalogue a place to hold what the engine actually reports, and make the value-state badge use it.

Purpose: today `gameDefault` is null on all 7 HUD toggles and on `citadel_hud_objective_health_enabled`. That has two consequences the requirement names. A stock line the engine wrote but Grimoire did not tag gets badged "Your override", which is a lie. And an unset toggle is badged honestly but cannot say what the game will do. `engineDefault` is the second field that closes both.

Note on merge cost: CONTEXT.md's standing constraint expects this to be a `performanceUserControls.ts`-only edit. It is not. `configKeyIndex.ts` and `src/types/electron.ts` each declare their own `ConfigKeyDefinition` that is kept in sync by hand rather than imported, and `computeConvarStates` builds a third separately-declared shape. The field must land at all four points or it never reaches the renderer. Every edit here is an added line beside an existing sibling line, which is the cheapest shape available at absorption time.

Output: `engineDefault` threaded end to end, the badge consuming it, and one new i18n key.
</objective>
