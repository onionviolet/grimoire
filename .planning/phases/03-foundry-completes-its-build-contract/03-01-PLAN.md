<assumption_delta_decision>
**Noun now primary:** the Foundry *edit kind*. `FoundryForgeEdit` stops being a two-member special case and becomes a kind-keyed family that `foundryForge.ts` dispatches over.

**Decision: `add-alongside`.** A third hand-written branch is added beside the existing two rather than a kind-to-builder registry. Rationale: CONTEXT.md D-03 locks the staged-edit shape to "a plain discriminated union member, matching sound/texture exactly", and the phase's standing instruction is "reuse existing mechanism over inventing new abstraction". A registry would be new abstraction with three entries.

**Accepted debt and its mitigation.** Add-alongside normally re-arms the `built[]` alignment trap for the next kind. This plan disarms it two ways instead of hand-waving it:
1. Every kind-branching site in `foundryForge.ts` becomes an explicit `if` / `else if` / `else if` chain whose final `else` assigns the narrowed `edit` to a `never`-typed local. A fourth kind added without a branch then fails `pnpm typecheck`, not silently at runtime.
2. A runtime invariant test asserts that a request holding one edit of every union member produces `built.length === request.edits.length`, so `built[i]` corresponds to `request.edits[i]`.

**What would force a later promote:** a fourth kind, or any kind whose builder needs a different cleanup lifecycle than "temp directory that gets removed" versus "shared cache that must not be". At two distinct lifecycles today the `if` chain still reads; at three it stops.
</assumption_delta_decision>
