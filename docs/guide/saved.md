# Saving mods for later

Use **Save mod for later** on any mod in Browse, then find it again in the **Saved** tab. Nothing is downloaded and nothing is installed: a saved mod is a bookmark.

This is the tab for the pile of mods you keep meaning to try. It is on by default, no toggle needed.

## Saving one

1. Go to **Browse** and open any mod.
2. Choose **Save mod for later**.
3. Open **Saved** to see it.

## Finding it again

Saved splits into **All**, **Mods**, **Sounds**, and **WiPs**, so a watchlist of fifty does not read as one list. **Search saved mods** filters within whichever section you are in.

**Sort** offers **Saved time**, **Name**, and **Recently updated**. Recently updated is the useful one for a long list: it floats whatever the authors have touched since you bookmarked it.

## Writing down why

Six months later you will not remember. **Edit details** gives you three fields:

- **Why did you save this?** The one worth filling in.
- **Notes**, for anything longer.
- **Tags**, comma separated, so you can search a theme rather than a name.

## Getting told about updates

**Watch for updates** on a saved mod marks it as one you care about, and its card shows **Updated** with the date when the author ships a new version.

## Taking your list elsewhere

**Export** writes your saved list to a file and **Import** reads one back. Use it to move a watchlist to another machine, or to keep it before a clean install. This is separate from [profiles](./profiles.md), which carry installed mods rather than bookmarked ones.

## If a saved mod says it is unavailable

The card reads **Saved mod unavailable** when Grimoire's local catalog copy has no entry for it. Usually the catalog just has not synced since you saved it. Open **Browse** once the next sync finishes and it resolves.

If it stays unavailable, the mod was probably taken down on GameBanana. The bookmark keeps whatever you wrote in **Edit details** either way.
