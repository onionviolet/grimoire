# Customizing how Grimoire looks

**Settings > Appearance.** Everything here saves as you click it. There is no save button anywhere in Settings.

**Grimoire is dark only.** There is no light mode and no theme toggle, so if you are looking for one, it does not exist.

## Accent color

The color used for buttons, links, highlights, and focus rings.

Eight presets: Ember (the default orange), Amber, Crimson, Rose, Violet, Azure, Cyan, and Emerald. The pipette swatch opens a picker for any color you like.

In the custom picker, dragging previews live but only **Apply** saves. Note that pressing Escape or clicking outside also commits rather than cancels, so use **Cancel** if you change your mind.

## Background glow

Tints the app background with light bleeding in from the top-left and bottom-right corners.

**No glow** is the default. Seven presets otherwise: Ember, Dusk, Abyss, Verdant, Nebula, Gold, and Ash. **Custom glow** lets you set each corner separately.

Same modal behavior as the accent picker: Escape and clicking away commit.

## Launcher and sidebar art

Four surfaces you can theme independently: the **Active tab**, **Launch Vanilla**, **Launch Modded**, and the **Volume bar**.

Click any of them and pick one of:

- **Default**, Grimoire's built-in look
- **Hero**, any hero's art, framed automatically with **Adjust framing** if you want it different
- **Custom image**, your own file, with the same cropper
- **None**, no background at all (not available for the active tab)

## Two layout switches

**Combine launch buttons** replaces the two stacked launch buttons with one, and you switch between Modded and Vanilla with the swap icon or a right-click.

**Transparent sidebar** drops the sidebar's dark panel so it sits flush with the page background. Icons, art, and the divider stay as they are.

## Language

Grimoire ships with English. Every other language is a community translation, downloaded the first time you pick it and cached so it works offline afterward.

The picker shows how complete each translation is. **System default** follows your operating system.

If you are offline, the list may be shorter than what actually exists, because it refreshes from the internet when opened. There is no message telling you that happened.

## Date format

`MM/DD/YYYY` or `DD/MM/YYYY`, applied to upload and update dates on mods and files. Each option shows today's date in that format so you can see which is which.

## Zoom

Not in Settings, but worth knowing: **Ctrl** with plus, minus, or zero zooms the whole interface, and it is remembered.
