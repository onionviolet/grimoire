# Managing skins in the Locker

Open a hero and click a skin to turn it on. Click it again to turn it off. This is the same enable and disable as the **Installed** tab, so a skin you turn on here is on everywhere.

## When you have more than one skin per hero

Only one skin can win a given file, so if two skins for the same hero are both on, one of them is doing nothing.

The **Load order** control on the Skins section fixes that. Drag to reorder, and **the top one loads first and wins**. This is the same rule as the rest of Grimoire, scoped to what you are looking at.

Reordering here reorders your real load order, not a Locker-only view of it. Nothing gets out of sync with **Installed**.

## Variants

Some mods ship several versions of the same skin: a red one, a blue one, one with a hat. Grimoire shows these as variants, and you pick which ones are on.

You can enable more than one variant if they change different files. If they collide, the same top-wins rule applies, and you can reorder the files inside the mod.

## The 3D preview

On a wide enough window, the hero portrait has a 2D and 3D toggle in its top-right corner. Flipping to 3D gives you a live model you can orbit, showing **the skin you currently have enabled**, or the default look if none is on.

It is a still pose, not an animation. Drag to rotate, and pause the spin if you want to hold an angle.

Some heroes cannot be posed yet and will say so. That is a limitation of the model export, not a problem with your skin, and the Locker falls back to the 2D portrait.

## Favorites

The star on a hero pins them to the top of the Locker grid, and keeps them visible even with **Hide empty** on. Useful if you mostly play three heroes.

## Shuffle on launch

**Shuffle on launch** equips a different skin each time you start the game from Grimoire.

1. Turn on **Shuffle on launch**.
2. Click the shuffle button on each skin you want in the pool.

Every launch, Grimoire picks one skin per hero from that pool. For mods with variants you can pin which variant the shuffle uses, or let it pick any.

Skins not in the pool are never chosen, so you keep control over what can appear.

## Locker images

The art Grimoire shows for a mod comes from the mod itself, but you can override it. Use the image button on a skin to set:

- **Card thumbnail**, the small image on the mod's card
- **Locker image**, the larger image in the Locker
- **Background**, the backdrop behind the hero

Pick from the mod's own images or upload your own, then frame it in the cropper. **Reset to default** puts the original back. If your image already has the hero's name in it, **Hide hero name label** stops Grimoire drawing a second one on top.

## Deleting

The delete button on a skin removes the mod and its files permanently. To keep a skin but take it out of the game, click it off instead.
