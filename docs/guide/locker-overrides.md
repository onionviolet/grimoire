# Reviewing and removing Locker overrides

Open **Installed > Locker overrides**. It lists everything the Locker has applied, across every hero, in one place.

Use it when you have applied things across a dozen heroes over a few months and want to see the damage, or undo it.

## Why a separate list

Locker overrides do not appear in your mod list. They are not mods, they take no load-order slot, and they do not count toward your 99-slot limit. See [what the Locker is](./locker.md).

That is convenient right up until you want to find them all. Without this screen, an ability color you applied to one hero months ago is only discoverable by opening that hero. This is the inventory.

## What is in it

Four tabs, one per override type:

- **Hero Cards**
- **Ability Sounds**
- **Ability Colors**
- **Trippy Skins**

Each lists the heroes you have applied that type to, and what you applied.

For ability colors it shows the actual pick, so a rainbow reads as a rainbow with its rotation rather than as a row of numbers. Trippy skins show whether they hit the body, the gun, or both.

## Removing

Remove any single entry from its row, or use **Remove all** to clear a whole tab at once.

**Remove all** is scoped to the tab you are on. Clearing every hero card leaves your ability sounds alone.

## Tuning without going back to the hero

Applied ability sounds keep their **Volume** and **Pitch** sliders here, so you can rebalance a sound that turned out too loud without navigating to that hero's Locker page.

Changes save on their own.

## Adding is still done in the Locker

This screen reviews, tunes, and removes. It does not apply anything new.

To add or change an override, go to the hero in the Locker. There is a link on each tab that takes you there.

## Restart to hear or see removals

Same rule as applying. Removing an override takes effect the next time the game starts, not in a running session.
