# Installing mods

Open **Browse**, find a mod, and click install. Grimoire downloads it, unpacks it, and puts it in your addons folder enabled and ready.

There are four ways in.

## From Browse

**Browse** is GameBanana's Deadlock section, inside Grimoire.

1. Search, or narrow with the **Filters** panel: **Hero**, **Category**, **Content** rating, and **Added** date.
2. Sort by **Popularity**, **Recently Added**, **Recently Updated**, **Most Viewed**, or **Name (A-Z)**.
3. Click a mod to open its details, then install.

Downloads run through a queue, so you can queue several and keep browsing. Archives (`.zip`, `.7z`, `.rar`) are unpacked automatically.

Grimoire keeps a local copy of the GameBanana catalog, which is what makes search fast and lets you browse when GameBanana is slow. It refreshes in the background.

## From a one-click link

Mod pages on GameBanana have a **1-Click Install** button. Clicking it hands the mod to Grimoire directly.

The first time, your browser asks whether to open Grimoire. Allow it.

## From a collection URL

To import a whole GameBanana collection, use the collection import on **Browse** and paste the collection URL. Grimoire queues every mod in it.

## From files you already have

To install a `.vpk` or an archive from your disk, go to **Installed** and click **Add local mods**.

Drag in any number of files, or browse to them. Names come from the filenames and you can edit them before importing.

One catch: drag the `.zip` itself, not a file you dragged out of an opened zip window.

## If Grimoire warns about suspicious files

Some archives contain files that are not typical for a Deadlock mod. Grimoire flags these and lists what it found.

Only `.vpk` files are ever extracted, so anything else in the archive is not installed either way. Still, take the warning as a reason to look at the mod's GameBanana page before continuing.

## Next

Installed mods show up on the **Installed** tab. From there you can enable, disable, reorder, and delete them. See [load order and the mod limit](./load-order.md).
